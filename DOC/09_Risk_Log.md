# 9. Risk Log

## 9.1 Purpose

Track anything that can delay, break, confuse, or weaken the project.

## 9.2 Template

| No. | Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | No real project files yet | High | High | Add actual working files before writing implementation docs | Open |

## 9.3 Rule

Do not hide risks in chat only. Put them here so the next person can see them immediately.
